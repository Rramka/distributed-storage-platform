# MVP Scope

Narrow but **complete** vertical slice. Validates secure distributed storage, orchestration, and automatic recovery.

1. [[User]] registration and authentication
2. [[Node Registration]] (certs, identity)
3. [[Client-side Encryption]] in [[CLI and SDK]]
4. Chunking and hashing
5. [[Metadata Service]]
6. [[Erasure Coding]] and distribution
7. Retrieval and reconstruction
8. Integrity ([[Storage Challenge]]s)
9. [[Self-Healing]] / [[Repair Loop]]
10. Health monitoring ([[Heartbeat Protocol]], [[Reputation]])
11. Basic billing — internal ledger only ([[Credits]], no real money) — **deferred on the solo track**
12. [[Admin Dashboard]] — **deferred**; fleet visualizer instead

The **solo builder track** ships items 1–10 (M0–M4) as the investable demo. See [[Milestones]] and [[Non-goals]].

See [[Non-goals]] for what is deliberately excluded, and [[Milestones]] for build order.
