# Credits

Internal unit of account. **1 CRD = 1,000,000 µCRD**. Integer math only.

Notional peg (e.g. 1 CRD ≈ $0.01) is a display concern and irrelevant to ledger correctness. Phase 1: balances accumulate; no Stripe, no payouts.

All [[Ledger Entry]] amounts are µCRD. See [[Map of Billing]], [[Customer Pricing]], [[Provider Earnings]].
